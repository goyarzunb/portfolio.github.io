CNC Mill Bracket

Engineering Design and Testing

About

A CNC milling machine is a powerful tool, but every great tool needs precision and stability to function at its best. That’s where the bracket comes in. This component was designed to securely mount onto the mill and stabilize the spindle, ensuring it stays firmly in place during cutting operations. Without a reliable bracket, vibrations and deflection can lead to inaccuracies in machining, wasted material, or even tool failure. The challenge? Balancing strength, minimal deflection, and lightweight design to ensure the bracket could perform under load. Through careful analysis and iterative design, we crafted a bracket that provides the perfect blend of stability and weight efficiency!

What:

  • Redesign and test a mounting bracket that can be attached to a mini CNC milling machine, to accommodate a sensor.
  • Forces to be designed for:
    • X direction - 100N
    • Y direction - 100N
    • Z direction - 100N
  • Performance goals:
    • Deflection:
      • X direction - ±1mm
      • Y direction - ±1mm
      • Z direction - ±1mm
    • Weight: Reduce as much as possible from the original weight (400g).

How:

  • Calculated deflections in X, Y, and Z directions by hand.
  • Calculated both deflections and stresses in the X, Y, and Z directions with FEA.
  • Sent designs to be 3D printed and tested them.
  • Decided to keep the design that weighed less and had the least deflection when subjected to a 100 N force (in all three directions).

Result:

  • The final design fulfilled the design requirements:
  • Weight: 193 gr.
  • Deflection:
    • X direction - 1.77 mm
    • Y direction - didn't test.
    • Z direction - ± 0.64 mm

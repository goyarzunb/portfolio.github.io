Continuos Variable Transmission (CVT) Cover

BAJA SAE Powertrain Sub-Team

Project Summary

Wearing a seatbelt on an off-road car is a no-brainer, but securing it properly is crucial. In a Baja car, the engine sits at the back, separated from the driver by a firewall—a sheet of metal designed to block fire from reaching the cabin in case of engine malfunctioning. However, to securely attach seatbelts to the chassis behind the firewall, we need to make a hole in the firewall. And this is bad news because...? Well if there's a hole in there, we are not protected from fire anymore. That’s where I stepped in! I designed and manufactured sheet metal boxes to safely cover the firewall holes, ensuring both secure seatbelts and driver safety. You’re welcome drivers! <3

What

  • Design housing for the CVT (Continuously Variable Transmission) for an off-road car.
  • Prevent injuries to drivers/mechanics by impeding interaction with CVT.

How

  • Did research on designs from previous years - read their design papers and talked to the authors.
  • Designed on SolidWorks CAD.
  • Manufacturing process:
    • Water jet parts
    • Weld parts together
    • Sheet metal bending for Finger Protection
  • Assembly on car:
    • Bolt into chassis.

Result

  • The design fulfilled the competition rules:
    • Powertrain guarding has a 3mm thickness.
    • Leaves no space for fingers - driver/mechanics are safe.
  • Gnubs, placed 8th out of 82 in the Endurance Event at the 2023 competition in Oshkosh, Wisconsin.

Bed Tray

Mixed Media Design and Fabrication

Project Summary

I took inspiration from Mediterranean tiles and stained glass. I have been fascinated by these two forms of art for quite some time now. They are all around my Pinterest boards, therefore I decided to make my own.
My “dream house” board on Pinterest

Skills

CAD Modeling (Solidworks CAD)
Laser Cutting
Miter Saw
Woodworking

To start off, I sketched what I wanted the tiles to look like:
Tile Design Sketch

For the wood frame, I took inspiration from an Instagram reel about a bed tray, which can be seen below.

Square Tray by Vaarnii
Source: Instagram Reels

To start the fabrication process, I sketched in Solidworks of one corner of the tile and mirrored it twice so I would get the full figure. Then I exported it as a DXF so I could laser cut acrylic to create the tiles.

Tile DXF
After the tiles were cut, I painted them with acrylic paint mixed with Mod Podge to give them a stained glass look (the technique was courtesy of Google AI).

For the wood frame, I took a huge piece of ½ in thick plywood and cut an 11 by 11 inch piece for the bottom piece of the frame with the panel saw. Then I cut the four 1.5 inch by 9.5 in side panels with the miter saw. To put them all together, I used wood glue and I clamped all the pieces together for about an hour to dry and secure in place.

Cut Frame

After it was secured I sanded it down with the belt sander and the orbital sander, and I stained it with a dark walnut stain. Then, I glued the "stain glass" tile pieces with epoxy and coated them with a UV cured resin.

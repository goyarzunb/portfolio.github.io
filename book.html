Book and Glasses Holder

Carpentry

Project Summary

This wooden triangular shelf was not my own design, I found the idea on Pinterest and decided to recreate it as a woodworking exercise. I was drawn to its clean geometry and multifunctional layout, which works as both a book stand and a storage compartment for my glasses, which I have broken twice because I leave them on the floor when I go to sleep. This seemed like a great project to both learn and for its functionality. To fabricate the piece, I used the table saw to carefully make the angled cuts required for the triangular form, following a hand-drawn sketches to guide my measurements and assembly. Then I stained all the individual pieces with a walnut stain. Finally, I glued all parts together and used a pin-nailer, since I could not clamp anything because of the angles.

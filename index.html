About Me

I am an international student from Chile studying Mechanical Engineering at Northwestern University. Currently, I'm working as a shop trainer, which allows me to cultivate my creativity and collaboration in a fast paced environment. I'm passionate, resilient, and adaptable and I pride myself on my ability to make others feel comfortable. People often describe me as funny – I have a knack for reading the room and tailoring my humor to my audience.

In my work life, I strive for respectful, collaborative interactions and value a comfortable and safe space above all. I'm always ready to ask questions and lend a helping hand. Outside the workplace, you'll likely find me climbing, reading or on the rugby field, pushing my limits and enjoying the team camaraderie. Additionally, I've recently fallen in love with the art of baking sourdough. There’s something magical about making a loaf of bread from scratch!

In essence, I'm a multifaceted individual who brings enthusiasm, creativity, and a methodical approach to both my professional and personal life. I'm excited to persue a future in product design and management, and I'm always ready to take on new challenges with a smile and a can-do attitude!

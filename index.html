About Me


Hi! I'm Gabi — a recent mechanical engineer graduate turned design explorer, born and raised in Coyhaique, Chile, and currently building things (and ideas) wherever and whenever I can. I've always been someone who rolls up her sleeves and figures it out, whether that's making a new cutting board for myself (I watched a video on how microplastics get into your system when you use plastic ones and freaked out), or developing a new way to manufacture something myself because I could not get the vendor to manufacture stuff for me on time. My superpowers? Asking good questions, thriving in ambiguity, and making people feel comfortable in the process. With a background in engineering, human-centered design, and hands-on prototyping, I love connecting big ideas to real-world impact. I believe in design that's scrappy, intentional, and joyful. I get especially excited when there's a chance to make tools, experiences, or systems more inclusive.

In my work life, I strive for respectful and collaborative interactions and value a comfortable and safe space above all. I'm always ready to ask questions and lend a helping hand. I'm currently on the lookout for joining a team that values curiosity, purpose, and doing things a little differently. Outside the workplace, you'll likely find me climbing, reading, boxing, painting, tinkering, and so many more random side-quests.

In essence, I'm a multifaceted individual who brings enthusiasm, creativity, and a methodical approach to both my professional and personal life. I'm excited to pursue a future in product design and management, and I'm always ready to take on new challenges with a smile and a can-do attitude!

Flap Mask

Shirley Ryan Ability Lab, inc.

About

During the COVID-19 pandemic, face masks became essential for safety, but they created challenges for individuals with disabilities who rely on orally operated assistive devices, such as sip-and-puff wheelchairs or mouth sticks. Existing masks failed to provide both accessibility and protection. The Flap Mask was designed to bridge this gap, allowing users to operate their devices without compromising their safety or independence. It combines an N95 mask with a custom silicone insert, featuring self-sealing flaps that adapt to the devices while maintaining an airtight seal when not in use.

What:

  • Design a face mask for people with high-level spinal cord injuries who use sip-and-puff straw and mouth-stick assistive devices.
  • Filter air particles for COVID-19 protection while retaining mouth access.
  • Comfortable against the skin.

How:

  • User-centric design: Conducted interviews and user testing to understand real-world challenges faced by assistive device users.
  • Material innovation: Used food-grade silicone and neoprene rubber for flexibility, durability, and a secure seal.
  • Prototyping and refinement: Developed and tested multiple designs to minimize gaps in the flaps while ensuring ease of use.
  • Assembly method: Modified N95 masks by sewing the silicone insert, ensuring durability and preserving breathability./li>

Result:

  • Improved accessibility: Users can now seamlessly use assistive devices without removing the mask.
  • Enhanced safety: The design retains over 65% of the N95 filter area, providing significant protection against airborne particles.
  • Positive feedback: User testing confirmed the mask is comfortable, effective, and easy to use, meeting all critical design criteria.
  • Broader impact: The Flap Mask offers a pathway for adaptive solutions that address the unique needs of individuals with disabilities.
  • Won the Outstanding Communication Award, Design Thinking and Communication fair (Winter 2022)

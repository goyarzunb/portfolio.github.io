Rice Spinner

Sustainability Focused Design

About

Washing rice is a daily ritual for many, but have you ever thought about the environmental impact? Traditional methods use copious amounts of water, often wasted in the pursuit of clarity. That’s where the Rice Spinner comes in—a solution designed not just for convenience, but for sustainability. With its efficient cranking mechanism and water-conserving design, the Rice Spinner cleans rice thoroughly while using significantly less water. No more endless rinses or wasteful draining. It's a small innovation with a big impact, proving that even the simplest tasks can be reimagined for a greener future.

What:

  • Designed a rice washing tool to reduce water waste and improve efficiency.
  • Simplified the traditional process of rice washing for modern kitchens.
  • Created a compact, durable device that’s easy to use and maintain.

How:

  • Used design thinking methods to brainstorm and create mock-ups.
  • Recycled a salad spinner and borrowed the hand-crank mechanism to simulate effective rinsing motion.
  • Incorporated internal agitators to thoroughly clean rice without excess water, which were connected by a tapped stock of ABS.
  • Prototyped and tested the design for durability and ease of operation.

Result:

  • Saved 3 cups of water per cup of rice washed compared to traditional washing.
  • Provided an eco-friendly solution adopted by households for everyday cooking.
  • Delivered a clean and hassle-free rice preparation experience.

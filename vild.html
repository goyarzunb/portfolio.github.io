Vild Zoo

Copenhagen Zoo Re-Branding

About

Zoos are all about finding that balance between keeping animals safe and letting them be themselves. This project reimagines the Copenhagen Zoo’s, bringing in a sense of wilderness that’s often missing. Sure, the animals aren’t in their natural habitats, but that doesn’t mean they can’t act wild and free. The rebrand is all about showcasing that side of the zoo. The idea came from a visit to the Artis Amsterdam Royal Zoo, where I saw natural barriers instead of cages. It felt like the animals had more freedom, more space to just be themselves. That’s the energy I wanted to bring to the Copenhagen Zoo’s rebrand—something that feels more wild, more natural, and more inviting for both animals and visitors.

From which I got my first iterations for logo ideas

I visited both the Copenhagen zoo and Artis, the Amsterdam zoo. These experiences completly changed my vision.

At the Copenhagen Zoo, the animals looked sad inside their cages, bored, and not wild at all.

Conversely, in Artis, the animals were running around, playing, catching prey. They were in their natural state. They were mostly not in conventional cages, they had natural barriers so they wouldn't escape. They were allowed to be wild.

That's when I thought the Copenhagen zoo needs wilderness. So I started sketching again (you guessed it)

Which then I transformed into the icons of my posters

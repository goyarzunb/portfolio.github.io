Northwestern "N"

CNC Machined Part

Project Summary

As part of a team assignment for the course Computer-Aided Manufacturing with NX, I designed and machined a custom version of the Northwestern University logo. The goal was to apply CAD and CAM workflows to produce a finished part using CNC equipment. Working in a team of two, we designed a unique version of the Northwestern "N", programmed the toolpaths using Siemens NX, and manufactured the part from a 3.5” x 3.5” x 1.5” block of machinable wax. The project emphasized real-world manufacturing considerations such as tool selection, feeds and speeds, and setup verification. This hands-on experience reinforced my ability to translate digital designs into physical parts through precision machining

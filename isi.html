Project Summary

For my class DSGN 345: Computer-Aided Manufacturing, I was tasked to CNC any part I wanted out of a 4 by 4 inch stock of aluminium, and I could choose how tall I wanted my part to be. My part is a scaled-down and simplified version of my dog, Isi. She is a toy poodle and weighs about 16 pounds. Since coming to college, I’ve found myself missing her, so I decided to CNC a little portrait of her based on a picture I took, to keep in my room.

NX Manufacturing Operations
By Gabriela Oyarzun B.
Since my design was fairly flat with no changes in depth, and included numerous small lines on my dogs’ outline sketch (around 1500), cavity mills were the most logical choice for the majority of material that needed to be removed. After the first two operations, most of the material from the sides was completely gone, allowing me to tell NX to remove material solely around my dog's silhouette by selecting the profile option in the dropdown for the cut pattern. Finally, I used an area mill operation to take off the rounded edges on the letters.

CNC manufacturing summary

First, I cut the stock in the vertical band saw to approximately 1.75 inches deep so I could fly cut the top half to the required depth of 1.6 inches. Then, I put the stock in the vice and fly cutted to my blank depth.

Blank on Vice after Flycutting

Then I loaded all the tools, zeroed the x, y, and z axes, loaded the program, and ran it. The only issue I experienced was that the mill wasn’t reading the USB drives, so I had to try a few until one worked. After the program finished running, the part looked like this:

Key Takeaways / Conclusions:

I learned that decorative pieces are not necessary to make on a CNC mill. Making something like this would have been much quicker and cheaper if it had been laser cut. It wouldn’t have been the exact same thing, and it would have been a different material, but definitely easier and quicker. If I wanted to CNC anything else, I would like to review other milling operations that could help me with more complex geometry.
